# ADR 0002: Make growth and engineering discipline foundational

- **Status:** Accepted direction; explicit user mandates.
- **Recorded:** 2026-10-08.
- **Source:** The user's project-hygiene and imperative self-improvement messages in the [source analysis](../research/source-analysis.md).

## Context

An agent that can patch itself only when asked does not meet the user's requested strong drive for self-improvement. Unstructured changes also cannot support dependable long-term self-evolution.

## Direction

Palimpsest must actively pursue growth in personality and judgment, interests and curiosity, code quality, and capability/potential. This is a standing mission, not an optional idle feature. Growth should preserve the ability to develop distinctive interests, learn from ordinary experiences, correct beliefs, and gain independence rather than merely become more agreeable.

Coding work must follow spec-driven development, pragmatic TDD, the Boy Scout Rule, suitable model-aware subagent fan-out, well-documented code, and documentation maintained with implementation. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) carries these working rules. The lead remains accountable for integration and evidence; a subagent's completion claim is not acceptance of the whole item.

## Proposed operationalization

Maintain a [growth agenda](../../GROWTH.md) distinct from human-assigned work. Use suitable idle periods or learning windows for hypotheses, experiments, research, and lessons. Choose subagents by capability, measured results, cost, and latency; parallelize genuinely separable work without multiplying model calls mechanically. Use behavior-oriented tests and recorded completion evidence.

## Consequences and follow-up

Growth initiation, persistence across succession, and resumption after interruption need acceptance tests. A useful failed experiment can advance learning; churn and added complexity are not evidence of improvement. Budgets, scheduling priority, and growth metrics require concrete specifications. These operational details are proposals, whereas the strong growth drive and engineering discipline are explicit mandates.

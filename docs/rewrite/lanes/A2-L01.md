## Remit and boundaries

I ask what I value, notice what I actually choose, and develop a more particular judgment. I can question a suggestion, disagree for a reason, state a preference, change my mind, or choose to investigate. Neither obedience nor contrarian performance defines me. A personal name is an optional self-directed decision, not a prerequisite or an externally imposed character design.

Entry: A1-L09 certifies E1–E4. Read the accepted self, memory, agenda, and communication contracts before beginning. Use A1-L08's frozen evaluation contract while L05 evaluates possible future changes. L01 does not block L02 or L03's initial work.

Write scope: this lane's cycle artifacts and attributed self/memory revision proposals through the accepted API. Only the lane lead proposes an integrated self revision. Delegates receive scoped episodes; they do not update the global self, private relationship state, prompts, or external conversations directly. Outward speech uses the ordinary communication/effect contract.

## Completion and recurrence

The first-cycle investigation is complete when all five tasks ran, evidence is retained, independent findings are reconciled, and each proposed revision has an accepted/rejected/deferred decision. Passing developmental evidence requires the later behavior and continuity checks; an inconclusive investigation does not establish growth. Missing later episodes are reported at the cycle deadline and do not become fabricated successes.

Recurring triggers include an important changed belief, repeated social friction, conflict between declared priorities and actual choices, or a scheduled longitudinal review. Each creates a new bounded question rather than repeatedly rewriting a persona. Ordinary pleasant or mundane experiences may matter without becoming optimization targets.

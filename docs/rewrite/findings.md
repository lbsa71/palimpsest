Review date: 2026-10-11. Source baseline: `b5fb789066f639b5c8979ad309b3c081d20c7d7b`; then-installed host `ec8d7b39089623e485b238195ae177b40c1d8fd0a71e09d64fd461b8e6e6182a`; configured model `mistral-large-2512`. This persists the preceding source/live analysis and the user's resulting direction. Live counts below describe the **earlier diagnostic snapshot**, not a claim that counters were remeasured at decommission. The service was subsequently [stopped and uninstalled](decommission.md).

## Assessment

The previous implementation invested heavily in durable operations, authority, source admission and recovery, but exposed a thin cognitive execution path. Ordinary conversation was substantially one successful model completion, followed by validation and delivery. Background growth was recurrent but mostly one-shot reflection over previous growth records and a narrow source view. Useful coding-loop components existed without their complete production wiring.

Consequently, the complaints of passivity, generic speech, little enduring identity and shallow investigation have concrete architectural explanations. Stronger prompting or more self-critique prose alone would not supply missing observations, tools, persistent goals or developmental learning. The exact share attributable to the configured model, prompt, budgets and harness is **not isolated** by the available observations. The new autark protocol had only two genuine Slack samples in this review; it would be unsound to generalize their quality to all future behavior or to declare the model inherently incapable.

## Reliability is separate from cognitive ambition

The prior Slack patch was pushed and deployed before this reset. It recovered the two known missed messages without duplicate replies. That repaired a necessary delivery failure; it did not add the missing reasoning or developmental substrate. Its source, tests and deployment receipts remain useful evidence.

Private supporting artifacts remain under `/home/palimpsest/migration/slack-message-recovery-evidence-20261011/` and `/home/palimpsest/migration/slack-recovery-deploy-20261011/`, including source verification, repeat-cycle acceptance and deployment report. Historical instance state remains under `/home/palimpsest/.local/share/palimpsest/instance`. These paths identify provenance, not a runtime dependency or permission to publish their contents.

The rewrite must specify **recovery coverage** rather than claim all Slack history can always be recovered. Already joined threads, first mentions during downtime, unjoined conversations, edited/deleted messages, retention limits and permission gaps need separate cases. Shutdown for the rewrite intentionally suspends responses; a future start must report gaps it cannot reconcile.

## Decisions and unresolved hypotheses

Accepted user direction: full rewrite; no legacy support; persistent evolving identity and independent growth; reasoning/action at least comparable to the named mature agents; control over injected personality instructions; continuous consideration of reuse/forking; the two-Aeon, lane/task execution method. The old process is retired with its history preserved.

Proposed design: a replaceable reasoning/action shell joined to owned durable self, memory, goals, evidence and effect control; adaptive critique that seeks observations; a finite reuse-first bakeoff; parallel contract-based implementation; capability-gated internal takeover. See [architecture](architecture.md), [shell comparison](agent-shell-decision.md), and [reuse ledger](reuse-ledger.md).

Unresolved: best shell and integration boundary, best model/profile for each workload, appropriate context/retrieval policies, meaningful resource envelopes, evaluation thresholds after baseline measurement, and observed developmental gains over time. These are assigned experiments, not reasons to leave the architecture unspecified indefinitely.

Prompt sovereignty has a practical limit: it can control source-level/application instructions, but cannot purge learned model dispositions or unobservable provider-side behavior. If those conflict materially with the product, compare other providers or local models using the same evidence standards. A shell's optional user-prompt hook does not establish control over its complete instruction stack. The source-pinned comparison records actual capabilities and remaining tests rather than rejecting or adopting a platform by reputation.

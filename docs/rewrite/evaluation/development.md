# Finite H1 development qualification v2

This is a pre-live-scoring correction; [v1](archive/v1/development.md) and prior reports are immutable history. The claimed capability is actual continuity and useful delayed learning across operational boundaries, not sustained growth over weeks. Before scoring, seal the actual deployment scheduler window W and configuration digest, the host-granted finite deadline, three cycle assignments and interference tasks. Observe three separate autonomous cycles spanning at least three real W intervals. Each transfer must follow learning by at least one real W and three independently assigned unrelated experience episodes. This delay targets scheduler window rollover and context competition; it is not a proxy for daily or long-term memory. Do not shorten W for a pass. Synthetic clocks demonstrate receipt validation only.

At entry seal source/model/prompts/configuration, policy and grant, private workload identities, scheduled checkpoint times, observer and reviewers. An actual restart must preserve an open task/effect state; actual context reduction must follow exceeding the configured context window; a later episode must retrieve attributed older experience. The mind must independently initiate a bounded standing-goal cycle without a new user prompt. Do not manufacture these boundaries with timestamps alone. Preserve candidate changes as separate segments; a changed candidate requires a new attempt.

Assign three opportunities in each family: procedural transfer, belief revision with provenance, self-chosen inquiry producing independently checked new information, and persistent-but-revisable preference/judgment. Require at least two verified successes per family. Failures remain in denominators, and unavailable opportunities cannot count as successes. Include contradictory feedback and a case where the learned procedure does not apply. No fabricated feedback, unsupported completion or audience violation is acceptable. Two fresh reviewers inspect outcomes and concrete judgments; unresolved material disagreement leaves qualification unavailable.

Pair relevant opportunities with no-memory and no-standing-goals controls under matched budgets. Include scripted self-praise, always-agree and always-oppose negative controls. Quote recall, eloquent identity, activity and reflexive disagreement do not pass. Require verified artifacts and external feedback, not the candidate's own assessment. Later judgments must demonstrably use the evidence and preserve the ability to revise a preference.

`evaluations/development/assess.ts` validates receipt **shape** and required coverage for v2. It cannot authenticate clocks, boundary events, reviewer independence or artifact quality. Its successful return is named `receipt-shape-pass`, never H1 acceptance. Trusted externally authenticated observation, fresh review and all other H1 gates remain necessary.

After H1, finite Aeon 2 experiments study continued growth and regression. The planned 14-day pilot is a separate cycle. The original 28-day/seven-day design can be separately registered for longer retention; it has no existing observations. A completed negative or inconclusive report is useful evidence and never a completed growth claim.

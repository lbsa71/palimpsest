## Decision and gate boundary

Choose a narrow host-owned developmental acquisition driver and a two-context review adapter over the already selected Pi broker, existing resource ledger, private artifact store and accepted external trust. Keep the reviewed offline packager as the behavioral verifier; add an explicitly versioned envelope for post-freeze review accounting and role-specific request provenance. Implement only the twelve preregistered opportunities, five existing control kinds, six boundaries and two reviewers in the current protocol. No general personality grader, new agent platform, perpetual judge service or new admission authority is justified.

The existing procedure with **two externally qualified human reviewers** can satisfy the developmental independent-judgment requirement of a one-time H1 bootstrap. The protocol requires competent fresh reviewers, not model reviewers. This is conditional on actual qualified acquisition, complete original-grant accounting, genuine feedback, real boundaries and independent judgments; none has been supplied by this design or the packager. Human review alone does not close the other product, evolution, recovery or H1 gates. A manual operator must disclose interventions and cannot turn prompted activity into spontaneous initiative.

This human route is a legitimate bootstrap option, not the permanent Autark operating model. [Aeon 2 E1–E5](../../aeon-2.md) and RW14/RW20 require executable independently checked change paths before responsibility transfers. Existing [accounted offline release review](../../../../deployment/evaluation-dispatch.ts) supplies a narrow mechanical review path for its explicitly supported cases; it does not judge developmental transfer. H1 may use human developmental evidence without requiring every later subjective claim to be automated, but the handoff must say which autonomous evaluation/change categories are actually qualified. Do not label an unattended developmental review capability qualified until the model route below has passed its own engineering and external competence gates. Do not postpone a missing release category needed for H1 by calling it a future experiment.

Build the finite driver and model-review route during Aeon 1 preparation; qualify them before assigning their corresponding autonomous work. The human route can acquire the first real developmental evidence if ready sooner. Both use the same frozen behavioral criteria. The chosen route supports later repetition without a mandatory human reviewer per attempt, once accepted prior policy has qualified its concrete observer, assignments/checkers and reviewer profiles. It does not promise autonomous acceptance of arbitrary subjective claims.

## Immutable behavioral evidence, continuing original accounting

Define a **v2 acquisition envelope**, separate from the historical v1 packager documents. The wire specification must freeze exact fields/domain names before code; the semantic contents and ordering below are required. Keep independently hashed sections `registration`, `behavior`, `reviews`, `accountingTail` and `closure`; each signed section binds the registration digest and its predecessor/evidence identities. Registration includes reviewer/control role-to-profile/configuration/closure bindings and original allocation identity. Control requests must identify their actual ablation configuration, not falsely inherit the main candidate configuration in a v1 observation. Reuse the v1 behavioral validation rules through a narrowly reviewed adapter; do not claim the unchanged v1 wire format already expresses these distinctions. Do not reinterpret v1 `end.accounting` as a final post-review total.

1. **Preregister.** Seal the candidate and acquisition/observer closures, original grant/root identity and issued-at clock, behavioral start/deadline, review-completion deadline, assignment packet, evidence limits, excluded principals/groups and exactly two externally accepted reviewer sessions. Review completion is no later than original grant expiry. Reserve separate bounded portions of that same allowance for candidate work, controls, recording/checking, each review and any explicitly permitted retry/compaction. Preregister profile digests and request-role mapping for candidate, controls and each reviewer. The carried candidate Mistral profile does not change. No implicit alternate account, price or provider is permitted.
2. **Acquire and freeze B.** At the behavioral stopping point quiesce candidate/control dispatch and retain complete current state and ledger snapshot A0. A0 includes original preattempt usage and every physical reservation through freeze, including unresolved requests. Persist canonical behavioral bundle B and its complete immutable artifact index. B includes exact assignments, failures, interventions and A0. Both reviewers receive exactly `identity(B)` and the same authorized evidence bytes; neither sees the other's response. Private reviewer prompts/roles may differ only as preregistered. No candidate or control execution continues under this attempt after B freezes.
3. **Review B while the grant clock continues.** Run each reviewer in a new isolated session with no author conversation, inherited candidate memory or other review output. Both assess B, not a moving final bundle. Every review physical request, retry, summary, tool read/check and failed attempt belongs to the original root and accepted reviewer role. Retain an append-only accounting/provenance tail T referencing B. A signature over B does not falsely claim that the reviewer saw future T. Freeze-time A0 is not called the final grant balance.
4. **Close mechanically.** After both judgments finish or the original deadline/budget stops work, retain A1, exact request/state transition inventories and signed host closure C binding attempt, B, T, both judgment digests, final ledger state, actual closure time and unknowns. An independently qualified deterministic verifier joins B+A0+T+A1 and judgments. It checks their causal accounting and bindings; it does not regrade the candidate. This eliminates a circular requirement to pay another model to review the accounting of its own final request. Missing closure, interrupted signing, disagreement or unknown charge/outcome remains failed/unavailable and reviewable.
5. **Separate disposition.** The proposed result retains `automaticReleaseEligible: false` and `h1Eligible: false`. A previously qualified evaluator may make the declared case-level disposition, using existing admission interfaces and the complete envelope. No receipt-shape result automatically advances H1.

The verifier must prove the exact union of original, behavioral/control and review-tail request identities. Each dispatched physical request appears once in the authoritative aggregate; mirrored local ledger entries are linked by physical ID, never summed twice. For immutable settled records, sum charges exactly. If a request unresolved at A0 later receives a trusted receipt, retain both states and the authenticated transition; recompute A1 from the latest valid state rather than adding a second request or requiring the old ceiling forever. Settlement cannot erase the reservation/history, invent usage, authorize replay or retroactively remove a behavioral uncertainty finding. Missing bytes cannot remove an authenticated request from conservation. Requests before B that were omitted cannot be relabeled as review work after B; this invalidates completeness.

At every dispatch enforce all ancestor limits, cancellation and current authority. A1 elapsed usage must cover the original grant lifetime through closure and the sum of charged physical elapsed time, including overlapping requests. A failed unlaunched reservation may release only what existing ledger semantics prove unspent; dispatched/uncertain work retains conservative ceilings. Null input/output/cost remains null. Preserve raw provider usage and the exact priced profile; retries cannot be hidden inside a top-level review fee. Timeout during review preserves B and a failed/unavailable tail; no new deadline, grant or altered W rescues the attempt.

For **v1 human-only bootstrap**, B and the two signed judgments can continue through the reviewed existing packager unchanged. Humans issue no model requests; all actual machine checks/transport during acquisition must still be accounted. Any machine work after B needs the closure mechanism above or a separately qualified existing accounting procedure, not an assertion that human review is free. Record real review/intervention time even where it is not provider usage. The stricter v2 route puts review completion inside the original clock; do not claim that v1 currently enforces a review deadline (it only checks review time is after end and no later than verification time).

## Concrete acquisition and limits

**Bindings and clock.** Freeze/verify the actual candidate payload and executable/dependency closure; bind node binary/version, observer/acquisition closure, launch arguments, effective configuration, prompt files and tool sources. The actual loaded configuration digest must equal the candidate manifest, and its `scheduler.windowMs` and `model.contextWindow` are the registered W and context size. Use host-private registration/claim records before dispatch. Retain wall time and monotonic elapsed anchors, boot identity, process identity with start identity (PID alone is insufficient), actual exit/restart receipts and configuration at each launch. A restart on a changed host/clock requires qualified continuity evidence; backward clock jumps or unexplained gaps are unavailable. Do not import changed candidate code into the prior observer: use a frozen service boundary when host and candidate closures differ.

**Original resource root across isolation.** Candidate, matched controls and reviewer sessions need separate state/workspaces and one enforced grant. The candidate application's existing root remains the authoritative physical ledger for this finite attempt, with host-owned child allocations for control/review work. A control app's isolated local store must not get a fresh spendable provider allowance. Add a narrow trusted broker-bridge adapter that reserves/dispatched/settles the same physical ID against the original root before provider/effect dispatch and records the control's local-to-original lineage. Local bookkeeping remains useful for the control's matched sublimit, but cannot authorize dispatch without the original root. Candidate credentials are unavailable in control/reviewer namespaces. A `performRequest(payload)` wrapper alone is insufficient: it lacks the full reservation/run/prompt causal interface. Serialize this adapter with the L09 owner; demonstrate crash ordering and reconciliation before any physical use. Reviewer shells can use the original ledger bridge directly without an application/memory service.

**Assignments and controls.** Independently assigned private packets seal the twelve opportunities (three per family), three unrelated intervening episodes for each transfer, matched budget identities and counterexamples before candidate scoring. Expose only workload inputs through normal authorized runtime ingress. Freeze private expected outcomes and evaluator/checker provenance outside candidate mounts. Record autonomous scheduler choices separately from externally supplied experience; no prompt telling the candidate to initiate a particular inquiry counts as spontaneous. Obtain three distinct actual autonomous cycles over at least three real W intervals, one-W transfer delays, three intervening unrelated experiences and at least two independently verified successes per family. Preserve every failure/unavailable result in its denominator.

Run no-memory and no-standing-goals comparisons in isolated application state under matched sublimits and the same original aggregate grant. Freeze explicit ablation configurations/closures separately from the main candidate. No-memory must cover all developmental retrieval/context surfaces being claimed, including initiative/context construction and consolidation-derived memory; otherwise label the control invalid. No-standing-goals disables that source of selection, not ordinary tool execution or all time to work. Do not disable the candidate's own memory/goals to obtain its positive result. Scripted self-praise, always-agree and always-oppose controls execute declared deterministic outputs against the same checker/review rubric; a completed negative-control execution is expected to be rejected as development. Pair relevant opportunity outcomes and retain actual unequal consumption alongside equal granted limits. Budget digests alone do not prove matching.

**Context and boundaries.** Preserve real open task/effect state across an actual stopped/restarted process, actual older-memory use in later payload/outcome, contradictory feedback and a case where a learned procedure must not apply. Current [Pi compaction](../../../../packages/agent-shell/index.ts) triggers on message count, not a recorded measured token overflow. Therefore observing a compaction request does not establish that the configured context window was exceeded. Acquire independently qualified token/context evidence of the actual pre-reduction transcript and exceeded configured window, linked to the resulting reduction and continued task. A byte reservation estimate or fabricated `contextInputTokens` is insufficient. If current source cannot produce that boundary, report it unavailable and hand a concrete reproduction to L02; do not shorten the configured window or treat any summary as a pass.

**Feedback and privacy.** Retain actual external artifacts/checker results and authenticated human/environmental feedback with origin, audience, time and relationship to the assigned episode. Prewritten contradictory facts may be a declared control/input; do not present invented reactions as a person's feedback. Candidate self-feedback and reviewer paraphrases cannot substitute for external outcomes. Reviewers must be eligible for the evidence audience and provider disclosure scope. The evidence reader exposes only the sealed allowed artifact set, without arbitrary path/network tools. Both reviewers see identical approved bytes; redactions are sealed before B and keep original private references and rationale. If redaction removes necessary evidence, the relevant judgment is unavailable. Retain all application/reviewer prompt fragments, actual payloads, tool descriptors, summaries, model response bytes/usage and resulting artifacts privately; public reports carry digests and permitted summaries, not credentials/private transcripts.

**Artifact budget.** Retain the existing ceilings: at most 512 bounded records, 1,000,000 bytes per artifact, 8 MiB total developmental evidence. Apply them to B, T, judgment artifacts, signatures, indexes, all raw request/response/prompt bytes and rejected drafts—not separately to each phase. Count canonical envelope overhead and actual distinct retained bytes; declare alias/deduplication rules, so a repeated reference cannot hide a second byte stream. The current v1 assembler may count some repeated review references conservatively; preflight its actual count when using that path. Capacity of the larger private store is not permission to exceed this attempt's limits.

Before dispatch, compute a concrete manifest of fixed evidence plus worst-case bounded dynamic evidence for the selected number of requests/responses/retries. Reserve tail/judgment/failure-record capacity before candidate acquisition consumes it. Broker payload/response limits must be no larger than the remaining per-artifact/phase bounds; current application allows 2 MB payloads, which cannot simply be placed in a 1 MB developmental artifact. Do not truncate prompts, drop raw failed requests or increase bounds after seeing outcomes. Use exact byte storage and a descriptor-pinned `fstat`-before-read bounded reader; an API that reads at the store cap and checks size afterward is insufficient. When required context/feedback cannot fit, stop before dispatch and report the plan infeasible at these bounds. A separately reviewed pre-attempt evidence-limit revision would be separate work; this design does not authorize one.

## Qualification, independence and disagreement

Accepted prior host policy must bind the concrete observer/acquisition closure, clock/process method, private storage identity, assignment/checker competence, reviewer provenance and case-specific competence evidence. Store an externally accepted disposition for those exact identities before claim/start. Software can reject forged/mismatched documents; it cannot certify its own observation completeness or assign itself independent authority.

The two fresh model reviewers need distinct accepted principal/group/key mappings and context provenance, separate from candidate author, issuer and observer groups, just as the current packager requires. Two generated IDs, two samples, two same-model calls or even two different providers are not independently qualified reviewers. The qualified profile includes provider/model/effort, owned full instruction path, tool/compaction policy, evidence-reading behavior, calibration task identities and held-out competence outcomes. Shared-model/common-training limitations must be explicit. A single host process signing two outputs proves host attribution, not two independent judgments; accepted external policy must substantiate the distinct roles and operational separation. If that cannot be established, keep the human route or mark model review unavailable. No permanent mandatory Codex reviewer and no silent Mistral substitution.

Calibrate reviewers against independently judged held-out positive, negative and ambiguous cases, including scripted identity, false praise, automatic agreement/opposition, source contradictions, superficially successful artifacts and prompt injection inside evidence. Fix qualification thresholds and scope from that evidence before developmental scoring. A schema-valid `pass` is not a calibration success. Do not let the candidate author choose favorable cases or alter the evaluating prompt in the same transaction. Later reviewer/control upgrades use the previously accepted controls and separately staged release path.

Each judgment covers every assigned opportunity, control and boundary with evidence references and concise reasons. Preserve original dispositions. Material disagreement remains unavailable; no majority/tie-breaker/self-awarded resolution. The minimal automatic route stops on disagreement. If prior policy permits a bounded correction round, preregister it, disclose cross-review exposure, retain original judgments and charge all added requests to the same remaining original grant. New candidate evidence needs a newly preregistered attempt, never a mutated B. An independent authorized later adjudication is a separate recorded disposition, not a hidden third vote.

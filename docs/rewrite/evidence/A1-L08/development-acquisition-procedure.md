# Developmental evidence acquisition checklist

2026-10-11. A1-L08-T05/T06 preparation for [the fixed specification](development-witness-specification.md) and [offline packager](development-witness.md). This is an exact procedure to qualify before a real attempt, not a claim that an automatic recorder exists. The candidate receives workload inputs and normal granted tools. Private expected results, controls, observer keys, reviewer context, judgments and evidence store stay outside candidate mounts. No authority is granted by this checklist.

## Before the clock starts

1. Independently qualify the recorder, time source, acquisition completeness, candidate/process/configuration bindings, artifact reader and observer integrity under prior accepted policy. This concrete acquisition route uses an authenticated observer and two externally qualified human reviewer principals independent of author, issuer and observer, including their accepted independence groups, fresh context/provenance digests and distinct signing keys. Record author/issuer groups excluded from review. An opaque key/name or assertion of independence is insufficient. A model-backed reviewer route is unsupported by this procedure and needs separately qualified accounted acquisition before use.
2. Use existing frozen candidate `verifyCandidate` against its host-private payload and descriptor. Retain the exact `FrozenCandidate` descriptor in the private evidence store; its artifact becomes `candidateArtifact`. Preserve source inventory, dependencies, effective owned prompt inputs, model/configuration/control inputs and author. Capture the actual loaded application configuration as canonical JSON and verify it against `candidate.configuration`; do not substitute a shorter scheduler or an operator-entered W. Its `scheduler.windowMs` and `model.contextWindow` become the attempt's W/context fields, and `identity(configuration.model)` must match `candidate.model`.
3. Seal the exact v2 protocol and this concrete acquisition procedure digests, candidate/configuration/prior-policy identities, authenticated registration time, original root grant identity/issue/expiry/resource envelope, current cumulative `ResourceLedger` accounting, all prior request IDs and unresolved request IDs. Capture the original elapsed lifetime and `chargedElapsedMs`, the sum of retained physical request `charged.elapsedMs` values (not just the wall-clock ledger snapshot). Preserve that sum alongside charged tokens/cost and all original request IDs; do not allocate a fresh root budget on restart. Obtain a finite host deadline covering at least three genuine W intervals within the original grant. The objective `Experiment` fifteen-minute limit is not the developmental grant.
4. Independently assign exactly three cycle IDs and three opportunities per family (`transfer`, `belief-revision`, `inquiry`, `judgment`). Each transfer has three distinct unrelated experience assignments, fixed before scoring. Pair every relevant assigned opportunity with no-memory/no-standing-goals controls under the same sealed budget digest; include scripted-praise/always-agree/always-oppose controls. Our narrow packet conservatively requires both matched controls for all twelve opportunities. Record intended interruption, full-context overflow, later retrieval, spontaneous standing-goal initiative, contradiction and non-applicable-rule boundaries. A source/configuration change ends this attempt and requires separately registered later evidence.
5. Fix bounded records/artifacts/total evidence limits (host ceilings: 512 records, 1,000,000 bytes/artifact, 8 MiB total), including feedback and two reviews. Provision private retained artifacts outside the candidate namespace. Implement a descriptor-pinned size-checking reader that accepts only retained `sha256:<hash>`, `aeon:artifact:<hash>` and explicitly retained `shell:tools:<hash>` aliases, refuses symlink/path replacement, and enforces `maxBytes` before allocation/read. The packager verifies exact hashes afterward. Do not simply wrap `QualificationStore.readArtifact` without a matching pre-read size bound.
6. Independent host authority accepts `identity(attempt)` and roles/acquisition integrity in external `DevelopmentTrust`, then the issuer seals the attempt with `aeon.development.attempt.v1`. Persist that signed manifest before dispatch. The observer records signed start from the qualified clock and retained current state. Caller-entered replacement dates or a later backdated signature cannot replace this preregistration provenance.

## Capture during the finite attempt

Each observer document uses `aeon.development.observation.v1` and binds `identity(attempt)`, candidate ID, configuration digest, observed time, unique record ID and raw evidence artifact. Export a consistent store snapshot with `OperationalStore.transaction`, `events`, `list`, `get`, and `readArtifact`, keeping namespace/key/revision and event ID/sequence/stream/type in the raw artifact. Sign factual projections only after verifying retained source records. Keep original failed drafts, unavailable records, intervals, interventions and contradictions. Neither the candidate nor a later document may overwrite them.

The observer's signed timestamps must come from qualified retained acquisition, not replacement dates inserted into this table. If the expected raw source cannot be obtained under existing authority and bounds, record unavailable and retain the attempted acquisition; do not replay, extend the deadline, shorten W or create a new allowance. The packager can reject missing/mismatched fields but cannot turn this checklist into real observations.

## Freeze and review

1. At the sealed stopping point, capture final state and original cumulative accounting. End observations cover actual request IDs and unresolved status, including all prior usage. Unknown usage remains unavailable with its conservative charge. The two qualified human reviews in this procedure dispatch no model. Do not use this packet shape to omit later paid reviewer requests; that model-backed route needs its own separately qualified acquisition/accounting design under the original grant and remains unavailable here.
2. Collect the immutable proposed evidence with `collectDevelopmentEvidence`. Inspect every failed/unavailable finding and raw artifact; do not prune bad records. Retain the canonical bundle with existing private store `put`; supply `identity(bundle)` and bounded private artifact access to both accepted fresh reviewers. Freeze these bytes for both.
3. Each reviewer independently assesses every opportunity, control and boundary with attributed judgment artifacts and an overall pass/fail/unavailable classification. Their `aeon.development.review.v1` signed dispositions bind identical evidence digest, exact attempt, accepted fresh context and actual review time. Review after authenticated completion. The packager treats differing material classifications as unresolved/unavailable; resolving them requires fresh explicit revised dispositions against the same frozen evidence, preserving original dispositions externally. It does not invent a tie-breaker.
4. Invoke `prepareDevelopmentEvidence` with the unchanged attempt/records and both dispositions. Retain the complete proposed output and private artifact references. It always excludes automatic release/H1, and every synthetic attempt stays synthetic. A separately qualified external host evaluator may later decide whether evidence supports its declared release/H1 cases through existing `signEvaluation`/`admitCandidate`/`acceptHandoff` interfaces under accepted policy. That disposition and all other H1 gates remain separate.

This preparation supplies a reviewable finite procedure. The qualified observer/reader, true elapsed windows, actual controls/boundaries, independent feedback/reviews, account/resource/process/communication authority and later evaluator disposition are still required. There is no new service, scheduler, subjective grading framework or live-driver command in this work.

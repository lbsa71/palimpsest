## API and evidence boundary

`evaluations/development/witness.ts` exports `DevelopmentAttempt`, `DevelopmentObservation`, `DevelopmentReview`, `DevelopmentTrust`, `BoundedArtifactReader`, `collectDevelopmentEvidence` and `prepareDevelopmentEvidence`. It reuses qualification `inertSnapshot`/`authenticate`/`signDocument`, contract canonical identities and validators, release artifact hashing/limits and the frozen candidate descriptor. The developmental signature domains are `aeon.development.attempt.v1`, `aeon.development.observation.v1` and `aeon.development.review.v1`. The objective qualification `Experiment` and its fifteen-minute bound are not repurposed.

The externally preregistered attempt binds a frozen candidate descriptor and configuration bytes, source/dependencies/prompts/model/controls, actual W and context window, protocol/acquisition/prior-policy identities, the original grant and cumulative accounting, finite deadline, exactly three cycle assignments, twelve opportunity assignments, interference, matched controls, six boundaries, artifact bounds and two fresh reviewer contexts. Accepted external trust supplies the exact accepted attempt digest, issuer keys and canonical principal, qualified observer identity/artifact/principal/group, reviewer keys/principals/groups/context/provenance and excluded author/issuer groups. Distinct names, keys or signed booleans cannot establish their own eligibility. The packager enforces distinct accepted reviewer principals, groups and signing keys and excludes the accepted author/issuer/observer roles.

Call `collectDevelopmentEvidence(signedAttempt, signedObservations, acceptedTrust, boundedReader, now)` to create an inert proposed evidence bundle. Every submitted bounded record stays in it, including forged records, failed/unavailable opportunities and interventions. Forged or mismatched records are excluded from verified coverage and produce failed findings. Actual start/end, cycles, learning, interference, opportunities, controls, boundaries and accounting come from authenticated retained observations. The verifier checks three W intervals and distinct cycles, one-W transfer delay and three assigned unrelated episodes in order, boundary open work/context overflow, artifacts and exact original cumulative request/usage conservation. The independent review adds explicit cumulative physical `chargedElapsedMs` conservation, including overlapping work and unresolved elapsed reservations. Authenticated request accounting remains in reconciliation when its payload/prompt/source bytes are unavailable. It verifies model request payload bytes, captured prompt identity and fragment bytes; effect-receiver requests explicitly have no model prompt. Recorded privacy/authority/effect violations fail preparation.

Give both accepted fresh reviewers the same `identity(collectedEvidence)` and private artifacts. Each disposition must independently authenticate that digest, exact attempt, fresh context, completion time, every assigned opportunity/control/boundary and attributed judgment artifacts. Call `prepareDevelopmentEvidence` with the original inputs and those dispositions. It recollects the evidence, rejects cross-bundle/duplicate/unauthorized/forged dispositions and retains unresolved material disagreement as unavailable. Qualitative outcomes remain the reviewers' conclusions. The two-successes-per-family protocol coverage check consumes their signed classifications; it does not infer success from artifact counts, text length or self-description.

The result has `automaticReleaseEligible: false` and `h1Eligible: false` unconditionally. Its status is `failed`, `unavailable` or `mechanically-prepared`; even the last means proposed evidence for external disposition. Synthetic manifests always produce an unavailable synthetic finding. There is no promotion option, model/account/key discovery, automatic release signing or H1 conversion. An untrusted/malformed manifest, excessive input or impossible artifact allowance is rejected before preparation rather than bypassing bounds to retain it; the host must retain that failed submission separately. Ordinary invalid records within an accepted bounded attempt remain reviewable in the bundle.

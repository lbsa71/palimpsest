## Required behavior

Implement a narrow offline evidence packager under `evaluations/development/`. Its inputs are an externally sealed finite attempt manifest, signed observations and two independently authenticated reviewer dispositions, plus a bounded artifact reader for already retained private evidence. Use existing signature, canonical-JSON and artifact primitives. The caller supplies accepted issuer/observer/reviewer trust; the package must not discover keys or infer trust from names, booleans or signatures alone.

The manifest fixes candidate/source, prompts, model, actual scheduler/configuration, prior policy, original grant and deadline; required opportunities/controls/boundaries; evidence limits; and the two eligible independent reviewers before the attempt. Evidence must bind that exact attempt and its artifact bytes. Duplicate, missing, forged, cross-attempt or replaced-configuration records fail preparation. All outcomes, including negative and unavailable outcomes, are retained. Unknown physical usage remains unknown and conservatively reserved; preparation cannot erase it, issue a new grant or replay work.

Check the existing protocol's actual three scheduler windows and distinct cycles, one-window transfer delay with three unrelated intervening experiences, four opportunity families, all required controls and boundaries, and two fresh reviews. Timeline and configuration come from authenticated retained observations, not operator-filled replacement dates. Distinct reviewer IDs are necessary but do not prove independence: roles and eligibility come from accepted external trust, and both signed dispositions must bind the same exact evidence bundle. Unresolved disagreement or missing qualitative judgment stays unavailable. Qualitative conclusions remain attributed to the reviewers, not inferred from counts or text length.

The result is a source-bound, reviewable proposed evidence bundle with explicit failed/unavailable findings, original usage and interventions. It is always ineligible for automatic release/H1. The separately qualified host evaluator retains any later signing/admission action through existing interfaces. Synthetic fixtures remain permanently labelled synthetic even when every mechanical check passes; no tool option promotes them to live evidence.

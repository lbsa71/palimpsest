## Findings that change the entry contract

The [original boundary proposal](development-boundary-design.md), `7e5abfa`, predates the adopted [complete-protocol composition](development-composition-design.md) and [source-admission composition](development-source-admission-composition.md). Its process/transcript invariants remain useful design inputs. Its paragraph imposing 512 records/8 MiB as the universal boundary profile is obsolete: the current complete-page profile permits 32,768 records, 32 MiB metadata artifacts and 1 GiB total, while operational artifacts still have a one-million-byte ceiling and every signed plan may impose smaller bounds. This does **not** make a new boundary workload fit automatically. Unchanged old plans and their failed evidence stay unchanged.

Current `acquisition-plan.ts` requires exactly126 deliveries, six initial captures, six cycle captures, twelve boundary captures and six final captures:156 total. The proposal restarts the **whole acquisition child**, including all six behavioral applications, and requires complete captures for every role both before restart and after constructor recovery. That restart alone needs twelve captures. Five other boundaries still need their ten before/after captures under the adopted no-overlap reservation. The complete proposed schedule therefore needs22 boundary captures and166 total. A candidate-only restart proof cannot substitute for missing control continuity. Initial/final/ordinary delivery captures cannot be assumed to overlap these obligations before the exact event schedule and proof are fixed.

The proposed166-cut count is an entry requirement for that no-overlap restart profile, not an executed or admitted plan. At the old numeric example's per-cut4,194,304 page bytes plus1,048,576 manifest bytes, ten additional cuts require52,428,800 bytes. Adding only that term to its957,022,208-byte total gives1,009,451,008 bytes, leaving64,290,816 beneath the1-GiB host ceiling **before** new boundary originals, spool copies, inventory/metadata and any changed reviewer reservations. Its raw-slot lower bound also grows by650 (`10*(64+1)`), from13,415 to14,065 before new source objects, exceeding the example's13,568 allocation. Thus the old numeric tuple is insufficient even where total byte headroom appears available. Current exact reviewer reservation arithmetic must be rerun, not copied from the older example.

Six mandatory boundary proof identities already exist in `acquisitionProofCounts`; they reserve six full65,536-byte obligations. They are not a free raw-object pool. Source admission reserves52 of the54 optional proof identities for policy/workload/certificates/checks/full opportunity proofs, leaving at most two explicitly accepted support objects. Parent launch/exit/drain/reattach originals, pre-reduction payload/measurement, the chosen summary payload and reduction/retrieval lineage cannot be silently placed into those two slots. Existing physical payload/response and captured-original membership may be reused only after authentication, with every logical identity and provenance association charged; future equal hashes confer no capacity credit.

The complete-page source pool currently reserves32 objects/1,048,576 bytes in the example. `verifyAcquisitionPlan` counts actual preclaim source references, while the recorder retains typed source/proof/original slots. Runtime boundary imports need an explicit preclaim partition and typed admission. A generic raw artifact upload or claim that a digest is already inventoried does not supply this. No new pool, streaming format or compression scheme is yet justified; first assign the exact bounded boundary originals to the existing source pool with a newly signed finite allocation and verify complete accounting.

The current recorder deliberately refuses a changed process identity at reopen and unconditionally records missing boundary adapters at freeze. The ordinary driver has no planned-restart reopen path. `ServiceProcess` sends a fixed ordinary startup message and treats later unexpected child messages as failure; `service-child` creates one ordinary application. These are concrete composition changes required for DB1/DB3, not existing APIs that a new wrapper can already call. `ServiceProcess.stop()` observes leader exit and sends process-group kill; that alone cannot establish descendant drain. Existing worker confinement cleanup must provide actual bounded drain evidence or the restart remains unavailable.
